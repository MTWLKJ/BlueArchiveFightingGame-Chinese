# Credits

The in-game Credits window (the Credits button on the title screen, or the C key) lists the same things. `scripts/ui/credits_panel.gd`
holds the list; a test keeps every link of it in this file.

## The game

Blue Archive Fighting Game (Tentative) is a fan project made by mattsumi. It is unofficial and has no connection with NEXON Games or Yostar.

- mattsumi on GitHub: https://github.com/mattsumi

## Music

Every track is from the Blue Archive Original Soundtrack, composed by Mitsukiyo, Nor and KARUT. The album covers in the jukebox are from its
releases (`assets/ba/jukebox/PROVENANCE.json`, rebuilt by `tools/jukebox_assets.py`).

- Blue Archive Wiki, Soundtrack: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Soundtrack
- Blue Archive Wiki, Original Soundtrack albums: https://bluearchive.wiki/wiki/Blue_Archive_Original_Soundtrack
